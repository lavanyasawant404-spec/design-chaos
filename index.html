Our Story

Kavad Art is inspired by the traditional wooden storytelling craft of Rajasthan. Each panel, motif and painted surface carries a visual connection to India's rich storytelling traditions.



Our collection reimagines this heritage through handcrafted objects designed for contemporary homes and everyday life.
