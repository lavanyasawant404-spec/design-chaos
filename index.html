Our Story

Kavad is a traditional storytelling craft from Rajasthan, where painted wooden panels are opened and displayed to narrate stories, legends and cultural traditions.


Kavad Art reimagines this heritage through handcrafted contemporary products, bringing traditional Indian storytelling into everyday life.


Every piece carries the spirit of craftsmanship, culture and storytelling.
